What it is

Dandelion is an AI learning companion for children aged 8 to 14 with dyslexia, dysgraphia and dyscalculia. It adapts to the child’s board and grade, speaks in their language, reads responses aloud, and is built never to make a child feel bad for getting something wrong. Parents get their own dashboard with progress summaries and safety alerts.

What I built

As co-founder and AI engineer, I built the adaptive learning platform using NLP, retrieval-augmented generation and personalized recommendations, supported by structured information-extraction and retrieval pipelines.

How it went

  • Deployed across three institutions for 50+ students.
  • Effectiveness reviewed by five educators.
  • Won 1st place at the Applied AI Challenge among 20+ teams, and received the Most Engaging Chatbot Award.
